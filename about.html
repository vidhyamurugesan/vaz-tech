Who we are

VAZ Tech Private Limited is a software engineering company based in India. Our team designs, builds, tests and supports software products end to end, from the user interface to the databases, search, AI and cloud infrastructure underneath.

We work exclusively with one long-term partner, an international software company. We don't sell products or take on outside projects. That keeps our attention where it belongs: on doing excellent work for the partner we have.
