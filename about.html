Who we are

VAZ Tech Pvt Ltd is a legal technology company focused on court data and litigation analytics. We help advocates, law firms and corporate legal teams track cases, monitor hearings and understand litigation through accurate, well-organised court data and practical analytics.

Every day, vital information is published across courts in the form of cause lists, orders, judgments and case updates. For most legal teams, keeping up means checking multiple sources by hand, often at the last minute. We started VAZ Tech to change that, by bringing this information together and turning it into clear, usable intelligence that helps lawyers prepare better and decide with confidence.
