03 Services

Case tracking and litigation analytics services.

Litigation success depends on knowing what's happening in court, and knowing it early. Our services bring together case data, hearing updates and litigation analytics, so your team can track every matter, prepare with confidence and plan strategy based on evidence, not guesswork.

TrackTracking and alerts

Case tracking and monitoring

Dashboard

Keep every matter in view, across every court. Add your cases once and see their current status, next hearing date, latest orders and full history in a single dashboard. No more checking multiple court websites or relying on clerks for updates. Your whole team works from the same accurate, up-to-date picture.

Hearing and cause list alerts

Email / SMS / App

Never be caught off guard by a listing. We monitor cause lists and case updates and notify you when your matter is listed, adjourned, transferred or disposed of. Alerts arrive by email, SMS or app notification, so the right people know in time to prepare.

New case and litigation alerts

Watchlist

Know immediately when a new case is filed by or against your client. Track the parties, companies and matters that matter to you, and receive timely alerts so you can respond early and protect your client's interests.

AnalyseLitigation analytics

Litigation analytics

Evidence

Move from instinct to evidence. We analyse court data to show how cases of a particular type tend to progress, how long they typically take, how often they are adjourned and how they are commonly resolved. Use these findings to set realistic client expectations, estimate timelines and plan your litigation strategy.

Court and bench trends

Patterns

Every court and bench has its own patterns. We help you understand listing trends, disposal rates and how similar matters have been handled before particular courts, so you can prepare your arguments with greater context.

Party and counsel research

Report

Before you advise, negotiate or appear, know who you're dealing with. Review the litigation history of a company, individual or opposing counsel, including past cases, outcomes and recurring disputes, all in one clear report.

Judgment and order research

Search

Find the authorities you need, faster. Search judgments and orders by keyword, court, date, case type or party, and use smart summaries to quickly identify what's relevant. Spend less time reading and more time building your case.
